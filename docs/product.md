# Shoots

> Learn to see like yourself.

> Shoot freely. Shoots turns each Shoot into evidence of what you are learning.

Shoots learns from every Shot, offers one optional personal Experiment, tracks what changes, and prepares a visual Deconstruction the photographer can keep or share.

## Who it is for

Shoots is for a self-directed beginner or post-beginner phone photographer. They know how to use a camera and recognise some Techniques, but they cannot tell whether a successful result came from a decision they control or from luck. They may take thirty Shots in one session, choose a few for Instagram, and leave the rest in the Camera archive.

They love photography without necessarily owning a dedicated camera or feeling entitled to call their phone work real photography. Community feedback such as "good picture" or "keep it up" feels kind but does not answer what to repeat. Asking an experienced photographer takes time, and embarrassment may stop the question before it is asked.

They want to shoot freely, explore more possibilities, feel proud of what they are learning, and compare current decisions with their own earlier work. They quietly quit an app that nags, grades, assigns homework, requires manual uploads, or turns every Shot into an essay.

## The problem

> I cannot see how my eye is developing.

The mechanism is distributed Evidence. Photographic control is not visible in one result. It appears across Shots, Scenes, Shoots, explicit attempts, conditions, and the decisions the photographer values. A strong Shot may be accidental. A repeated Technique may still be unconscious. A community sees the result but does not remember enough history to distinguish recurrence from deliberate reproduction.

Single-Shot critique apps forget the next day. Asking a general model requires uploading many files, repeats the same one-Shot limitation, and produces long prose without a learning record. A Camera archive grows without showing what the photographer tried, varied, repeated, or learned to make on purpose.

Shoots answers:

> Which photographic decisions am I making repeatedly, which can I now make on purpose, and how is that changing across my Shoots?

## What quantifiable means

Shoots does not collapse photography into one skill or quality score. It keeps distinct evidence about individual decisions:

| Evidence | Supported statement |
|---|---|
| One corroborated sighting | This Technique appeared once. |
| Sightings across distinct Scenes | This Technique keeps recurring. |
| Explicit Reproduce attempt | The photographer tried to make this decision deliberately. |
| Reproduce Criteria met | The declared decision was reproduced in that result. |
| Reproduction across different Scenes or conditions | The decision held outside one situation. |
| Positive Keeper count | This decision appears in Shots the photographer marked as valued. |
| Comparable distribution difference | This behaviour changed between exact earlier and later sets. |

One occurrence cannot distinguish control from luck. Recurrence cannot prove Intent. A Keeper cannot prove repeatability. These figures remain visible rather than being hidden inside a grade.

## The work

After one source permission, Shoots intercepts ordinary Camera media and performs the learning bookkeeping no occasional mentor will maintain by hand:

1. It imports every unseen Camera Shot without an upload or Analyse button.
2. It measures and reads each Shot while the photographer keeps using the normal camera.
3. It groups capture-continuous Shots into Scenes and Scenes into one natural Shoot.
4. It waits until every member Run is complete or terminal.
5. It records what repeated, what varied, which Techniques appeared across Scenes, and which results the photographer marked as Keepers.
6. It updates the Technique Map, Tendency Profile, comparable Change, and Journey.
7. Scout chooses whether to explain supported work, ask one consequential Intent question, offer Explore, offer Keeper-backed Reproduce, or stay silent.
8. It verifies explicit Experiment results using type-specific Evidence.
9. It prepares a concise Shoot Record and optional Deconstruction draft.

A human doing the same work would inspect every Shot, reconstruct sessions, compare decisions, preserve exact baselines, remember previous advice, examine later results, and lay out a shareable explanation. Shoots performs that work in the background.

The work leaves checkable artifacts:

- a Run for every accepted Shot;
- a Shoot Record with exact Scene and Shot membership and terminal coverage;
- the Technique Map and Tendency Profile;
- a type-specific Experiment Record;
- comparable Change and a Journey Update;
- an optional Deconstruction draft.

Advice without those artifacts is not the product.

## The learning scale

The product works at four related scales:

- **Shot** is the atomic media and Evidence unit.
- **Scene** shows how one photographic situation was worked.
- **Shoot** shows what the photographer explored across a natural Camera session.
- **Journey** shows how decisions change longitudinally.

Shot detail is evidence, not the main learning report. The primary reflection happens after a meaningful Shoot. Thirty isolated critiques are not a Shoot reflection.

A useful Shoot receipt may say:

> You photographed four Scenes. In three, you made one framing and moved on. In the last Scene, you tried three distances and two viewpoints. Backlight appeared again, and this was the first explicit attempt that reproduced its declared edge-light Evidence. Your marked Keeper came from the closest Variation.

## The loop

| Step | Shoots does | Artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Intercept | Phone Source receives unseen approved Camera media by stable source reference. | Shot arrival ActivityEvent |
| Observe | Ingest measures pixels and metadata; Analyst records bounded visual Evidence and labelled reads. | Analysis |
| Assemble | Capture continuity assigns Shots to Scenes and one Shoot. | Shoot membership |
| Settle | The Shoot waits for every member Run or terminal unreadable outcome. | Terminal coverage |
| Synthesize | Code and one bounded batch read describe repeated and varied decisions without choosing a best Shot. | Shoot Record |
| Remember | Cartographer updates the Technique Map, Tendency Profile, Change, and Journey. | Longitudinal records |
| Choose | Scout explains, asks, offers Explore, offers Reproduce, or records evidenced silence. | Stored Scout decision or Experiment Record |
| Verify | Explore records Variation observations; Reproduce checks fixed Criteria; Compare preserves alternatives and preference. | Type-specific Experiment evidence |
| Share | Scribe renders one-claim visual pages from stored Evidence. | Deconstruction draft |

The photographer only has to shoot. Keeper, Intent, Experiment participation, and Mine versus Inspiration on manual import are small optional authority signals that let Shoots speak more honestly.

## Three kinds of Experiment

**Explore.** Ask what changes across two to four optional Variations. It records what the photographer tried, what visibly differed, and which results became Keepers. There is no correct result, pass, fail, or Verdict. Explore is the next Experiment type after the Shoot-level record because it expands knowledge without taking over the camera.

**Reproduce.** Deliberately repeat a pattern associated with the photographer's Keepers. Criteria are frozen before the result, and Judge may issue a Verdict about those Criteria only. A Verdict is not a quality judgment.

**Compare.** Change one variable, preserve both alternatives, and let the photographer say which result they value. The model does not choose the winner.

The current implementation proves Reproduce. Corrected Explore, Shoot-level synthesis, and Compare remain implementation work. An unavailable route produces silence rather than a mislabeled substitute.

An Experiment is optional. The camera starts free, only explicitly selected Shots join, and the photographer may pause or leave without failing anything.

## The honesty line

Quality is an opinion. Behaviour is measurable.

Shoots keeps three claim classes separate:

- Measurements may state what happened and what changed within their actual signal.
- Models may offer interpretations and Moves, labelled as model reads.
- Only the photographer supplies Intent, Keeper preference, Mine versus Inspiration, and the signal that a Change felt like improvement.

A Keeper is positive-only. Unmarked means unknown, never disliked. A slow shutter may establish elevated shake risk without proving the cause of every soft pixel. Subject-area share cannot prove that a Shot lacks a centre of interest. A proxy reaches only the claim it actually supports.

When Evidence is weak, Shoots says less. The panel may abstain. Scout may stay silent. Journey may name a blind spot instead of inventing a conclusion.

The sentence competitors do not say:

> Shoots does not score your photographs; it shows which photographic decisions are becoming yours.

## The phone source and daily client

The photographer keeps using Android's normal camera. Shoots asks once for honest media access, filters to approved Camera media, uploads unseen Shots in the background, and reports what was imported, skipped, retrying, or unreadable. Selected-media permission provides manual import only. Full access enables automatic future imports.

Android is the daily client. The web remains the deeper ActivityEvent and Run audit desk; both read one Photographer record.

- **Now** shows the active Camera or Capture Session state, then the newest settled Shoot receipt or meaningful result.
- **Shots** is the visual archive and evidence audit.
- **Experiments** owns Explore and Reproduce offers, exact references, Capture Sessions, and earlier Experiment Records.
- **Journey** owns Tendency, Technique Map, deliberate-reproduction evidence, and Change across time.
- **Settings** owns permissions, Drive, notifications, device sessions, and account actions behind progressive disclosure.

The earlier custom camera, Scene Probe, weather context, and Gemini Live direction remain later Companion work. They are not required for the unattended Shoot-to-learning-record proof.

## Image-led learning

One Shot read keeps four parts together without becoming a report:

1. a supported decision worth noticing;
2. one measured Finding or labelled panel observation;
3. one specific Move;
4. one visible condition to look for on the next attempt.

One layer speaks at a time. Camera and subject Moves teach the next capture. A tested crop is a secondary way to inspect the existing Shot. Generic grids are shown only when they explain recorded geometry; several grids stacked after the fact are not proof of composition.

An explicit Reproduce result remains separate from the general Shot read. A strong Shot may not meet unrelated Criteria, and the Criteria result may never overwrite what the Shot demonstrably does well.

## Inspiration

Approved Camera media defaults to the Photographer's work. A manual import that might be someone else's asks one small question: Mine or Inspiration?

Inspiration may be studied through Technique, structure, light, colour, depth, and moment layers. It may seed a personal Explore. It never enters the Photographer's Technique Map, Tendency Profile, Keeper evidence, Change, or Journey as their work.

## Deconstruction

Shoots may prepare an Instagram-ready carousel or another shareable Deconstruction after a meaningful Shoot or Experiment:

1. a photographer-selected Keeper cover;
2. composition structure;
3. light or colour decisions;
4. Scene Variations or a contact sheet;
5. deliberate-reproduction Evidence;
6. comparable Change and an optional caption.

Each page carries one claim and one visual layer. If no Keeper exists, Shoots may prepare evidence pages but asks the photographer to choose the cover. Shoots never posts automatically.

## Why agents belong here

The difficult work is not generating critique. It is choosing the justified learning operation, carrying it through, evaluating what happened, and changing future behaviour.

Models handle visual ambiguity and language. Versioned domain code owns source authority, Technique ids, grouping invariants, thresholds, corroboration, Experiment semantics, Criteria, barriers, comparability, and Change.

The agent system must:

- refuse unsupported claims;
- ask only when one answer materially changes its action;
- synthesize a Shoot rather than repeat thirty Shot descriptions;
- plan Explore or Reproduce from longitudinal Evidence;
- remember exact Shot, Scene, Shoot, Experiment, Intent, Keeper, and constraint records;
- evaluate its intervention rather than artistic quality;
- revise or retire advice only after repeated comparable outcomes;
- leave a visible artifact and audit trail.

More critic personas, model calls, grids, or prose do not create this depth.

## What Shoots refuses to become

- a single-Shot score or critique queue;
- a chatty rules recital;
- a course, streak, or skill tree;
- a social feed, editor, generator, filter, or culling tool;
- a professional manual-camera replacement;
- an Inspiration feed that mistakes references for the Photographer's work;
- a hidden location tracker;
- an agent that chooses the shutter, Keeper, taste, or social post for the photographer.

Generated reference clips and Director remain outside the core.

## Product test

A successful demonstration shows one ordinary Camera period becoming one completed learning record without upload, tagging, or pipeline controls:

> It intercepted the photographer's Camera stream, grouped the Shots into Scenes and a Shoot, accounted for every Run, showed what repeated and varied, chose one justified learning action or silence, updated the Journey, and prepared a visual Deconstruction.

Then an explicit Experiment shows whether a declared decision became reproducible without claiming the resulting Shot was better.

If a viewer only says "it gives photography advice," the product has not been made visible.
