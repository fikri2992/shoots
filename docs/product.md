# Shoots: what you tend to shoot, and what to try next

**One-liner.** Photography apps judge single shots. Shoots remembers all of yours — and shows you, with numbers you can check, how your eye is changing.

**The sentence no other product says.** *"Your keepers are 3× more likely when you shoot below eye level. You do it 7% of the time."*

**The question it answers.** *What kind of photographer am I becoming, what do I naturally lean toward, and am I actually improving?* Single-shot critique has no memory; it can explain one frame but can never say what you repeatedly notice, which techniques have become reliable, which choices you never explore, or whether anything has changed in months — and the hobbyist's quiet fear is exactly that: *I might take 5,000 shots and remain the same without knowing it.*

## Who this is for

The hobbyist, not the professional. Someone who shoots on walks and trips because they enjoy it, whose photos all somehow come out the same, and who would like them to feel more *theirs* — but who will quit anything that feels like school. That audience sets the voice everywhere: the app describes leanings before it corrects anything, it suggests rather than prescribes, it celebrates before it critiques, and the deepest thing it offers is not a curriculum but a mirror — *this is who you are as a photographer so far, and here is somewhere you have never gone.* Improvement is the product; joy is the constraint.

## Every update says three things

One rule governs every surface — the pulse on the phone, the review on the web, the Journey Update. (Vocabulary is decision 39: a **Tendency** is a neutral, evidence-backed pattern; **Progress** is measured change; a **Verdict** is only ever a quest's pass/fail and nothing else borrows the word.)

1. **What you lean toward.** The tendency this shot continues or breaks. Descriptive, never an accusation: "you like centred, quiet frames," not "you repeat safe decisions."
2. **What to improve.** One thing, with its figure. Kind, concrete, checkable.
3. **Where you've grown.** The progress this shot proves: the skill tick, the pair against your own older photo, the leaning that widened.

### The Journey Update — the finished artifact

The product's real deliverable is a paragraph, produced when the profile meaningfully moves:

> *"You consistently notice isolated people in quiet spaces and use muted colour. Backlight has become repeatable across five shots. Your framing has moved closer since March, but your camera height barely changes. Try one low-angle walk if you want to explore that boundary."*

Every clause is anchored — to a count, a corroboration, or a distribution shift — and the reader can check any of them. That paragraph is what "an evidence-backed record of the photographer's eye and development" looks like on screen, and it is what the hobbyist actually wanted when they installed a photography app: not a quest ticket closed, an honest answer about who they are becoming.

### What can honestly be tracked

| Progress type | Can Shoots measure it? |
|---|---|
| **Exploration** | Yes — variance widening across light, placement, distance, time, techniques |
| **Reliability** | Yes — independent evidence repeatedly corroborating a technique (decision 33) |
| **Change** | Yes — the recent distribution against the earlier one |
| **Artistic improvement** | Not by itself — the model's score is not a substitute |
| **Personal style and taste** | Only from the photographer's own **Keeper** marks (decision 40) |

Without Keepers, Shoots can honestly say *"you changed."* With them, it can say *"you are moving toward what you value."* It can never say *"you improved"* on the model's opinion alone — and it doesn't.

## The problem, stated precisely

Boring photographs do not come from weak equipment or wrong settings; they come from **passive decisions** — the same safe choices made again and again without noticing. Across a coded sample of twelve professional and educational sources (`boring-shots-advice-research.md`), gear was central to one; the recurring advice: decide what the shot is about (7/12), work the scene (6/12), simplify to one subject (5/12), wait for the moment (5/12), better light (4/12), give yourself a constraint (4/12), edit hard against the alternatives (3/12).

The advice is not secret — "move closer" is on every listicle. The problem is that **nobody can see which safe decisions they repeat, and no critique turns into different behaviour on the next shoot.** Generic advice bounces off; scores bounce off harder — the corpus shows the rubric's five elements are one number (r = 0.986 against `impact` alone) and rule-of-thirds compliance barely predicts quality (d = 0.36). Scoring is a boring-detector wearing a rubric costume, and formula-following is itself a cause of sameness — the pros' counter-advice says so.

What the pros actually run is a loop: notice your defaults, set a constraint against them, study someone who solved it, work the scene, edit hard, repeat. That loop is deliberate practice — specific task, edge of ability, immediate feedback, repetition — and photographers cannot run it alone, because the noticing, the honest feedback and the hard edit are exactly the parts that need a second pair of eyes.

Shoots is that second pair of eyes, and it never sleeps.

## The thesis

**Quality is an opinion. Behaviour is measurable.**

No system can honestly measure whether a photograph got better — that stays the model's opinion, argued by a three-lens panel, vetoed by arithmetic where arithmetic reaches, every fault carrying the figure it was computed from. But whether the *photographer changed* is arithmetic on data already on disk. So Shoots makes its checkable claim about the photographer, not the frame — "arithmetic, not opinion", extended from the frame to the person. Models propose; pure code verifies; where nothing can be measured — the moment, whether the picture *lands* — the prompt says so and the model keeps the last word.

Put as labour, coaching is three different jobs. **Perception** — looking at a photograph and describing it — is cheap now; an LLM does it and it is nobody's product. **Judgment** — saying whether it is good — is unreliable by our own measurement (one number wearing five bars, r = 0.986), so Shoots deliberately does not sell it. **Bookkeeping** — measuring every frame exactly (~40 facts per photograph: fourteen tone measurements off the pixels, twelve EXIF fields, the derived exposure and sun arithmetic, subject position and share, faults with figures), remembering all of it across years, and checking afterwards whether anything changed — is the job humans are worst at and agents exist for. A 500-photo archive is ~20,000 maintained measurements. **Shoots sells the third job — as a mirror, not an audit.** Nobody can see their own leanings — not "won't", *cannot*: a tendency is only visible in aggregate, and no human reads their own archive in aggregate. The friend who has seen everything you ever shot and can tell you who you are as a photographer does not exist, because that work is exactly the work nobody will do by hand. The agent does it, tirelessly, and every number it produces can be re-derived from the file it came from.

## The loop

| Step | What happens | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| **1 Detect** | The Tendency Profile: every shot a point in decision-space, tendencies = the low-variance dimensions | arithmetic |
| **2 Assign** | The Scout turns the least-explored dimension into an optional challenge, citing the count — and, where Keepers exist, the payoff | agent |
| **3 Study** | One master's photograph (Commons) that solves that dimension, attached to the quest with one sentence | agent |
| **4 Shoot** | The native camera: pinned constraint, live measurements, scene counter, variation meter, summonable coach | arithmetic + agent |
| **5 Verify** | The panel reads the work — for a worked scene, it *ranks the alternatives* and argues the keeper — and the tendency counts are recomputed: did behaviour move? | both |
| **6 Compare** | This shot against your own previous best; the recent distribution against the earlier one; when the profile meaningfully moves, the **Journey Update** is written | arithmetic + agent |

Then the loop feeds itself: new counts, next challenge. The agent is not a critic with a queue; it is a coach writing challenges from your own photographs — and a hobbyist recognises the shape at once, because weekly photo challenges are already how hobbyists play. The difference is that these ones are *about you*.

### 1 — The Tendency Profile is a decision-space

Each shot records where the photographer stood in every dimension that is measurable: **height** (IMU pitch, in-app shots), **distance** (subject share of frame), **placement** (`subject_x/y` against the guides), **light** (hour against NOAA sun position), **dwell** (frames given to the scene), **subject type**, **moment vs. static** (the model's read). A tendency is a dimension with low variance — you explore distance, but height is a constant. It is neutral by definition: a repeated centred frame might be laziness or the beginning of a style, and Shoots does not decide that alone. When the photographer marks **Keepers** (decision 40), the profile gains the taste axis: *your own* keeper rate correlated against each dimension, so a challenge is not generic advice but *what you value, underexplored* — and the divergence between what you do often and what you keep is the most personal sentence the mirror can say.

### 2 & 5 — The Scene is the unit of critique

The pros' process is scene-shaped, so the domain grows a level: **Shot → Scene (frames of one place and time) → Session**. For a worked scene the panel does not score a frame; it **ranks the alternatives and argues for the keeper** — which is where judges are actually reliable (ranking beats scoring by +42, `research-findings.md`). The contact-sheet edit, the step amateurs never do and cannot do alone, becomes something the app runs with the photographer. A single stray shot still gets the classic review.

### Intent is first-class, never required

One line, spoken or typed, before or during: *"about the loneliness of the bench."* Given intent: the panel judges against it rather than against generic goodness (which is what "decide what the shot is about", the top advice at 7/12, means operationally); measurements are excused by it — a declared silhouette mutes the zebras, a declared centred stillness ends the thirds talk; and intents accumulate into clusters, from which the agent proposes **projects** — "twenty treatments of one bench" — rather than only weekly constraints, holding up a mirror to an emerging style. No intent given, nothing is asked.

### Graduation — the scaffolding comes down

Once a technique is solid (corroborated, three times, decision 33), the coach stops mentioning it, forever, and says so once: *"you don't need me for horizons anymore."* The product's endgame is its own silence — an app whose decreasing chattiness is the proof it works. Retention is identity, not streaks: Journey shows tendency entropy widening, before/after pairs, projects finished. *I can watch myself becoming a photographer.*

## Two surfaces, split by tempo

**The camera is the fast, live half of the agent** — everything that answers while a decision is still open:

- Zebras over blown highlights (same CLIP_HIGH = 250 as the backend, mirrored tests) — *built*
- Guide overlay and subject placement — the guide, never the cell mesh (decision 28)
- IMU pitch — the eye-height tendency, measured at last
- Scene frame counter + **variation meter**: spread across this scene's frames — "you have varied height but not distance"
- Golden-hour countdown (NOAA arithmetic)
- The pinned constraint, with its master's reference one tap away
- The **summonable** coach: tap or ask, one Gemini line anchored to a measurement; uninvited at most once per half minute, only when one move clearly wins
- The verdict pulse ~30 s after the shutter: praise with proof first, fault second, skill tick. The moment of levelling up lands in the hand that took the shot.

**The web is the async, deliberate half** — everything worth a chair:

- Full review: guides, fault figures, the Feldman critique; the contact-sheet ranking for worked scenes
- The Tendency Profile and its movement, shoot over shoot; the Journey Updates, newest first
- The **Keeper** mark on any shot in the grid — one tap, optional, the only taste signal (decision 40)
- Journey's hero: before/after pairs against your own previous best
- **The agent's desk**: the live pipeline feed from ActivityEvents — measurements landing, three lenses filing, the vote, a veto firing, the Scout issuing the next constraint

The shutter uploads into the same ingest path the Drive watcher feeds (decision 35). The camera is a second door into the same brain.

## Principles

1. **Silence is a feature.** A voice correcting every frame is a backseat driver. Summoned coaching is the interaction; graduation retires it.
2. **Praise first, with proof.** The strongest corroborated technique leads; faults follow with their figures.
3. **Intent is respected.** An app that fights the photographer's intent thinks *it* is the photographer.
4. **Progress is shown, not gamified.** Your own photo from a month ago beside today's. No streaks, no guilt.
5. **Ask nothing.** No tagging, no setup, no rating the review. Shoot; it works.

## What Shoots refuses to be

A scoring app. A rules recital. A per-frame nag. A culling utility. A school with homework. A generic assistant that says "improve your composition" — the advice tier the twelve sources exist to rise above.

The market already sells "Photo DNA", "Style Fingerprints" and progress claims (`market-agent-comparison.md`). The differentiation is not the promise, it is the epistemics: transparent evidence a user can re-derive, tendencies kept neutral, no opaque quality score anywhere, and a hard wall between measurable change and personal taste — crossed only by the photographer's own Keeper marks, never by the model's opinion.

## The agents: what is already deep, and what depth is still owed

The governing thesis is **models perceive; the domain reasons.** A glued-LLM product keeps its expertise in prompt text — unversioned, untestable, unfalsifiable. Shoots keeps it in `domain/`: thresholds, prerequisites, excuses, vetoes, promotion rules — code that can be replayed over the corpus and *proven wrong*, which has happened twice and been published both times (solid skills fell 16 → 6 when the evidence bar rose; see decisions 33–34).

Five properties already separate the agents from glue, each visible in code and tests, not claimed:

1. **They can lose arguments.** A measurement vetoes the panel however confident the lenses were; the overruled claim survives as recorded dissent (decision 34).
2. **Belief requires corroboration.** Nothing enters the skill map on one lens's word (decision 33) — and imposing that rule demoted ten of the system's own conclusions.
3. **Authority is partitioned.** Disjoint element and technique ownership; measurements routed only to the owning lens; the Synthesizer alone sees everything because it writes the paragraph (decision 31).
4. **They fetch their own evidence.** The scrub lens pulls the exact frames the Composer named; a proposed crop is proven on pixels before anyone sees it (decisions 23, 29).
5. **Autonomy carries a citation.** The first quest is issued ~90 seconds after signup, unprompted, and every quest names the observation that caused it (decision 26).

What is still owed — an expert coach does five things this system cannot yet do:

| Verb | An expert does | Today | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Refuse** | "I can't call this one — show me more" | the panel always produces a verdict | no confidence floor, no abstention |
| **Escalate** | attention proportional to difficulty | the same four calls for a trivial and a contested frame | no cheap-pass → full-panel path |
| **Plan** | a season, revised on evidence | `choose()` returns the next technique | no persistent plan |
| **Remember** | what makes this person quit | `missing_gear` + free-text notes | no structured learner model |
| **Self-grade** | notices its own advice isn't landing | nothing | no feedback loop on the coach itself |

The two that define the product are **self-grading** (decision 37) and **abstention** (decision 38): the first makes the coach an agent that runs experiments on its own advice, the second makes it one that knows the edge of its competence. Both are pure domain code, testable against the corpus. Escalate, plan and remember are the roadmap behind them.

## What work is accomplished

A hobbyist has two questions they cannot answer alone: **"am I actually getting better?"** and **"what should I try next?"** Tendencies are only visible in aggregate, progress needs an honest outside witness, and nobody in their life is doing that work. The work Shoots accomplishes is answering both questions continuously, with artifacts — and the agents' depth is the same answer from the other side: a hobbyist cannot audit an AI critic, so the critic audits itself. Measurements veto it (decision 34), beliefs require corroboration (33), it abstains when unsure (38), it checks whether its own coaching worked (37), and every claim carries its figure. The architecture is not decoration next to the problem; it is what the audience forces.

The full list of work the agent does **unprompted**, each item leaving a verifiable artifact. The test every row passes: *what would a human have to do to produce this, and can they check it?* — hours of tabulation to produce, one photo's arithmetic to verify:

| Work | Trigger | Artifact it leaves |
|---|---|---|
| Reads the photographer's existing portfolio and measures it | connect Drive | measurements + faults per shot, on disk |
| Builds the Tendency Profile and names the weakest dimension | corpus read | the profile, with counts anyone can recheck |
| Writes a personal training plan — constraint quest with evidence and payoff | profile change, daily tick, quest closed | the quest, citing its numbers |
| Researches the quest: technique context, a master's reference | quest issue | the Scout's brief + Commons image |
| Coaches live in the viewfinder, on request, anchored to measurements | summon | the line, with its figure |
| Judges every shot: three lenses argue, measurements veto, faults carry figures | shutter | the review, in Drive and on the web |
| Runs the contact-sheet edit on a worked scene: ranks, argues the keeper | scene detected | the ranking with reasons |
| Updates the skill map on corroborated evidence only; promotes; **graduates** | verdict | SkillState transitions, audit trail |
| Compares against the photographer's own previous best | verdict | the before/after pair with one sentence |
| Verifies its own coaching worked: recomputes tendency counts after the shoot | ingest complete | the profile diff — behaviour moved or it didn't |

The last row is the one that matters: **the agent checks whether its own intervention changed anything, with arithmetic, and shows the diff.** Work assigned, work done, work verified — the full Taskmaster arc, and every step of it audited by figures rather than claimed.

### The demo, in those terms

1. Projector: the agent reads the corpus; the Tendency Profile appears — *centred 12/18, eye-height, 1.2 frames per scene, zero golden-hour shots*.
2. The Scout issues the constraint with its evidence; it lands pinned in the viewfinder in hand, master's reference attached.
3. Live shoot on stage: zebras, scene counter climbing, variation meter filling; the coach is asked aloud and answers with a figure.
4. Shutter → the agent's desk lights up: measurements, three reads, the vote, the verdict — the pulse hits the phone: *"keeper — low angle corroborated."*
5. The tendency count on the projector ticks. **Behaviour moved, and the number proves it.**

## Build order, by value

1. **`domain/tendency.py`** — decision-space over stored measurements, keeper correlation, tests over the corpus. The product's spine; pure; no model calls.
1b. **The Keeper** — one field on Shot, one tap on the grid and the phone gallery. Smallest change with the largest honesty gain.
1c. **The Journey Update** — the profile-diff trigger in code, one writer agent for the paragraph, every clause anchored to a figure the diff provides.
2. **Shutter → ingest** — the camera joins the brain.
3. **Scout reads the profile** — quests cite counts and payoff. The Taskmaster beat.
4. **Verdict pulse + pinned quest** on the phone — the loop closes in the hand.
5. **Scene entity + contact-sheet ranking** — the biggest domain change, the biggest critique upgrade.
6. **Viewfinder live measurements** — IMU pitch, scene counter, variation meter, golden hour.
7. **Summonable coach line** — Gemini, TTS, anchored.
8. **Intent** — capture, excusal, panel contract change.
9. **Graduation** — small rule on the skill map, large soul.
10. **Study step** — Commons curation attached to quests.
11. **Agent's desk polish, Journey hero** — the demo surfaces.
12. Architecture rows 1–5 (`architecture-findings.md`) run before any of it — they are hygiene, not features.

## Honest limits

- The Tendency Profile counts only what is stored: height exists only for in-app shots; the profile gets richer as the camera is used, and says so.
- "Meaningful variation" is measured as framing spread — a proxy for variation of *idea*; the meter is honest about which it measures.
- The 12-source synthesis is a purposive sample — direction, not proof. The measured half of the product is the counts.
- Whether a constraint made the photographs less boring is the model's opinion at Compare time, presented as exactly that.
- Keeper-rate correlations start meaningless at n = 18 shots and say so until the corpus grows; the profile shows counts before it shows claims.
